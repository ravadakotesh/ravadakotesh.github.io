CNC PLATE ASSEMBLY

Project Overview

The CNC Plate Assembly is a precision mechanical assembly designed to support accurate positioning and clamping of components during machining operations. My contribution involved reverse engineering the existing assembly by capturing critical dimensions, developing accurate 3D CAD models, and creating a complete assembly with detailed engineering drawings. The project required careful interpretation of component interfaces and assembly relationships to produce production-ready documentation for manufacturing and future design modifications.
