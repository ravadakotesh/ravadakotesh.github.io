Booster Rotor

Project Overview

The Booster Rotor is a precision-machined rotating component used in a booster system to efficiently transfer mechanical energy for fluid or gas movement. The project involved reverse engineering an existing component by developing an accurate 3D CAD model and detailed manufacturing drawings from the available part. Careful interpretation of the component geometry and machining features was required to create a production-ready design that accurately represents the original component and supports manufacturing.
